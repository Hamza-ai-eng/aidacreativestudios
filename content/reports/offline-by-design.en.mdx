## Executive Summary

This report documents how Palestinian refugees — 5.9 million people registered across Lebanon, Jordan, Syria, and the West Bank — are systematically excluded from the AI tools shaping life in 2026. Excluded not by accident, not by oversight, but by the same architecture of dispossession the AI industry is contracted to sustain.

We trace the exclusion across six dimensions: electricity (intermittent across all four host states), payment infrastructure (no banking in most refugee camps; OpenAI does not accept the local payment methods that exist), contracts (the Terms of Service that no Palestinian refugee can sign), language (the dialect the model was never trained on), geography (the IP block that returns "service not available in your region"), and accountability (the absence of any legal framework under which a Palestinian refugee can contest a model's decision about them).

Each dimension is a deliberate choice. Together they form a system: the Palestinian refugee is kept offline by design across four states and four legal systems. Different mechanisms. The same result.

This is the first of two reports. The companion series — *Offline by Design — The Series* — is a six-essay set extending these arguments through specific cases.
